# howellfamilyeyecare

> **Unofficial copy — not the website of Howell Family EyeCare.** This repository is a development copy of
> a redesign, published so that the redesign can be looked at in a browser. It is not operated by, affiliated with, or endorsed by
> Howell Family EyeCare. The practice's real site is https://www.howellfamilyeyecare.com/.
>
> **A snapshot, with no form of the practice.** The pages were read from the practice's site on the evening of October 25, 2026, Central Time (October 25, between 23:18 and 23:27 UTC), and are
> not kept current. The live site showed no form of the practice on its form pages when they were read, and this copy has none; its only forms are the site search.

**Preview: https://chris-sgen.github.io/howellfamilyeyecare/**

A **redesign** of **howellfamilyeyecare.com** (Howell Family EyeCare, an eye care practice in Pryor, Oklahoma): 354 pages, made by a rebuild pipeline and changed for public hosting as listed under *Hardening*: the 351 pages of the live site that the crawl found, at their own addresses, and a search page, a sitemap page and a not-found page that the build makes itself. It is a static site with no backend. To look at it, use the preview link. To serve the folder yourself, use a server that answers a folder's address (`/insurance/`) with the `index.html` inside it, under the path `/howellfamilyeyecare/` (the not-found page names its files there).

> **8 still pictures and 1 silent video loop in this copy are AI-generated illustrations made for this redesign.**
> They are not photographs of the practice, its office, its doctors or its staff, and none shows a person (see *Generated imagery*).
> **12 snapshots of people from its posts are not shown here** (Hardening 6). The portraits of the practice's two optometrists, and the photograph at the head of the home page, are shown as on the live site.

Two terms used below. The **private build** is the build of this redesign that this copy was made from,
with its documentation and audit files; it is not published. This **preview** is that build with the
changes listed under *Hardening*.

## Hardening applied to this public copy

`PROVENANCE.json` gives the counts.

1. **`noindex, nofollow, noarchive, nosnippet, noimageindex` on every page.** This tag is the only index control this copy has. It is a request, not a lock: the large search engines say they follow `noindex`; `noimageindex` is a rule of Google's. A `robots.txt` with `Disallow: /` is also shipped, but it has **no effect** here: crawlers read `robots.txt` only at the host root (`chris-sgen.github.io/robots.txt`, which only a separate repository for that host could provide), not under `/howellfamilyeyecare/`. GitHub Pages cannot send an `X-Robots-Tag` header either. That leaves every file that is not a page (the pictures, the loop, the fonts, the stylesheets, the script, the search index, the licence texts and these documents) with no index control of its own; on this site they are linked only from pages marked `nofollow`. The repository itself is public: github.com lists every file of it, and its page there can be indexed.
2. **Link-preview tags.** `og:url` points at this preview, `og:description` / `twitter:description` carry the disclosure, `og:site_name` reads "Unofficial preview (not Howell Family EyeCare)", and `og:title` / `twitter:title` begin "Unofficial preview:" (354 pages), with the practice's name taken off where it stands at the end of the title after a bar or a dash ("… | Howell Family Eyecare"); a title that names the practice inside its own words keeps them. The home page's own title is "Welcome to - Howell Family Eyecare", which would leave "Welcome to"; its card title keeps the words whole, without the dash ("Unofficial preview: Welcome to Howell Family Eyecare"). The picture tags (`og:image`, `twitter:image`, `og:featured_image`: 763 tags) are removed and `twitter:card` is `summary`, the small card, so no tag offers a picture. The practice's icon is not this copy's tab or home-screen icon (708 icon links removed); an empty icon stands in. **The `<title>` of a page is not marked**: it is the private build's title. What a link card shows depends on the app: apps that take a link's description or site name from these tags show the disclosure; apps that read none of these tags show the page's own title with the `chris-sgen.github.io` address; apps that pick a picture from the page itself may show one of its pictures.
3. **JSON-LD removed** (1,059 blocks). It asserted, as data for search engines, the practice's identity, address, telephone and opening hours, and the trail and kind of each page.
4. `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` on every page, so that, in browsers that honour the tag, a click on an outbound link does not tell its target this address. The 353 map frames carry `referrerpolicy="no-referrer"` themselves, because a policy on a frame overrules the page's. No page holds a film (see 7); the script's code for a film frame (unused here; it would load `youtube-nocookie.com`) would send only this site's bare origin.
5. **No form of the practice, here or on the live site.** The live site's 5 pages named for a form (`/contact-form/`, `/contact-us/appointment-request-form/`, `/contact-us/contact-form/`, `/contact-us/patient-registration-form/`, `/for-vendors/`) held only their title when they were read, as saved and as drawn in a browser, and this copy's hold the same; this copy adds no form. ## Known limits
