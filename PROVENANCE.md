## Pictures and video made for this redesign

8 stills and 1 loop (one more still was made only as the first and last frame of the loop), made on the evening of October 25, 2026, Central Time (between 23:58 UTC on October 25 and 00:06 UTC on October 26). None shows a person, the practice's office, a clinical result, lettering, a label, a logo or a named product. Every still was opened whole and then in enlarged regions at its full size, looking for lettering, numbers, marks that read as a brand, a recognisable product, a person or a hand, a malformed object and a border; the loop was looked at as a sheet of four of its frames (0, 40, 80 and 120) and its middle frame (60) at full size. Each still was made in two candidates, and the better one used. The files here are re-encodings made by the private build (WebP through a browser canvas, H.264 without an audio track); each carries the AI label (README, Generated imagery) and was read for metadata before publishing (README, Verification). ## Pictures from the live site

249 file names of pictures published on the practice's site are used (305 counting each version: mostly the different cuts the redesign makes of one picture): photographs and drawings of the pages' topics, the portraits of the practice's two optometrists, a photograph at the head of the home page that appears to show Dr. Brett Howell with a woman and two children, and a photograph of the inside of an optician's shop (both on the home page, as on the live home page), the pictures and logos of the makers and insurers its pages name, the logos of payment cards and badges for other ways to pay, and the EyeGlass Guide's logo. One of them, a red eye on `/hours-location/`, has a file name that begins "ChatGPT-Image": it is the live site's picture. 6 banner photographs, whose files on the live site carry a dark or grey veil laid on them for the lettering set over them there, are shown with that veil taken off (a levels change made by the private build). Most pictures stand on the page where the live site shows them; the redesign also shows a page's picture on the cards and menus that lead to that page. 12 snapshots of people from its posts are not shown here (README, Hardening 6). 5 picture addresses that live pages name are not on their host (it answers "not found", HTTP 404: 2 on `da4e1j5r7gw87.cloudfront.net`, 3 at the live site's own address), so those pages cannot show them on the live site either, and the rebuilt pages do not show them; 19 more addresses on the live site's own host, which it answers "not found", are named only in the link-preview tags of its pages: those pages show the same pictures in their text from another host, and this copy carries 13 of them from there (the other 6 are snapshots of Hardening 6).
